### 🚨 Don't Choose a Sign Before Considering Its Location

One of the most important questions to ask is:

**Where will people see this sign?**

A sign positioned next to a busy road has different requirements from one installed inside an office reception area.

Before choosing your signage, consider:

✔ How far away will customers be when they see it?

✔ Will they be walking or driving past?

✔ What direction will they approach from?

✔ Could trees, vehicles, buildings, or other signs block visibility?

✔ Does the sign need to be visible at night?

✔ Is the surrounding area visually crowded?

✔ Does the installation location have any specific requirements?

The location can influence the sign's size, materials, colours, lighting, and installation method.

---

### 🎨 Keep the Message Clear

A common signage mistake is trying to put too much information on one sign.

Your business name, logo, slogan, phone number, website, social media details, services, promotions, and other information can quickly make a sign difficult to read.

Remember that customers may only have a few seconds to process your message.

For many outdoor signs, the most important information should be immediately recognisable.

✔ Business name

✔ Logo

✔ Essential contact information where appropriate

✔ Short supporting message if required

✔ Consistent brand colours and fonts

The goal isn't to say everything.

The goal is to make sure people notice and understand the most important thing.

### 🧱 Choose Materials Based on the Application

The material used to manufacture a sign can affect its appearance, durability, maintenance, and suitability for its environment.

Common signage materials include:

✔ Acrylic

✔ Perspex

✔ Metal

✔ Vinyl

✔ Wood

✔ ACM and composite materials

Each material has different characteristics.

**Acrylic and Perspex** can create a clean and modern appearance for corporate, retail, and interior applications.

**Metal** can provide strength and durability for signs that require a robust finish.

**Vinyl** is commonly used for window graphics, promotional branding, vehicle applications, and other printed signage.

**Wood** can provide a natural or traditional appearance for businesses looking for a warmer visual style.

**ACM cladding** can be used for contemporary building facades and architectural signage applications.

The right material depends on where the sign will be installed, how it will be used, the desired appearance, and the expected lifespan.

### 👀 Consider Viewing Distance

A sign can look perfectly readable when you are standing directly in front of it but become difficult to read from the road.

This is why viewing distance matters.

When designing signage, consider:

✔ Letter height

✔ Font selection

✔ Colour contrast

✔ Spacing

✔ Overall sign size

✔ Viewing angle

✔ Traffic speed

✔ Distance between the sign and the viewer

The design should reflect how people will actually encounter the sign.

A sign intended to be read by pedestrians can be designed differently from one that needs to be understood by drivers approaching from several hundred metres away.

### 🧠 Questions to Ask Before Ordering Business Signage

Before starting a signage project, it helps to understand exactly what you need.

Ask yourself:

**1. What is the main purpose of the sign?**

Is it for branding, advertising, navigation, safety, visibility, or a combination?

**2. Where will it be installed?**

Consider the building, surroundings, traffic, viewing angles, and available space.

**3. How far away will people see it?**

This will affect the size and readability of the design.

**4. Does it need to be visible at night?**

If yes, illuminated signage may need to be considered.

**5. What materials are appropriate?**

The environment and expected lifespan should influence your material selection.

**6. Does the sign need professional installation?**

Large, outdoor, illuminated, or building-mounted signage often requires experienced installation.

**7. Are there safety or compliance requirements?**

Certain businesses and locations may require specific types of signage.
